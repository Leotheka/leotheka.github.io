Welcome

I'm Leonardo. This is where I keep what I've been thinking about the world: questions I can't let go of, half-baked ideas, and a few conclusions I'm still willing to change.

I write to understand. Many of these notes are born somewhere between engineering, teaching, and everyday conversations, and I share them because thinking out loud has always been better in good company.

There's no rush. Read whatever calls to you, keep what's useful, and if something moves you, I'd love to hear about it.
